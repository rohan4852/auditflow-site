Product & service guide

Compliance evidence mapping, with human review

IsoFlow helps compliance professionals organize potential evidence from policy documents against a control framework such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2. The current offering is a scoped, human-reviewed service—not a publicly available API.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Current availability: IsoFlow currently offers evidence-mapping engagements by discussion. Public API access and self-serve engine subscriptions are not available. Future workspace tiers will be announced when ready.

1. What the service helps with

  • Review policy and procedure documents for statements that may support control requirements.
  • Organize potentially relevant evidence against a supplied control matrix.
  • Preserve source references, such as document text or page references, where available in the source and output.
  • Prepare structured results for a reviewer to inspect and refine.

Results depend on the source documents, their structure and quality, the control matrix, and the agreed scope. Not every document will contain evidence for every control.

2. Typical engagement workflow

Step 1 — Scope the work

Discuss the framework, document set, desired output, control matrix, confidentiality requirements, and review needs before submitting materials.

Step 2 — Analyze documents

Documents are processed to identify text and statements that may be relevant to the agreed controls. AI-assisted processing may be used as part of this workflow.

Step 3 — Human review

Potential matches and source references are reviewed within the agreed scope. The level of review and any limitations should be confirmed in the engagement description.

Step 4 — Deliver reviewable results

Results may be provided in a structured spreadsheet or another agreed format so your team can verify source references, assess evidence sufficiency, and decide next steps.

3. Outputs and source references

Depending on the agreed engagement and available source information, results may include a control reference, a potentially relevant evidence excerpt, a source document or page reference, and reviewer notes. These fields are not guaranteed for every source or every control; confirm the required output before work begins.

IsoFlow's outputs are intended to support verification. Customers and qualified reviewers should check quotations, page references, control interpretations, and evidence sufficiency against the original documents.

4. AI processing and data handling

The current demo may use Google Gemini for AI-assisted processing. A paid-service architecture using AWS and Amazon Bedrock is planned, but is not represented as deployed. The provider and configuration used for a specific engagement should be confirmed before sensitive documents are submitted.

Review the Security Overview and Privacy Policy. Do not submit sensitive client evidence until you have reviewed the processing arrangements and confirmed you are authorized to share it.

5. What the service is not

  • It is not a public API offering or a promise of direct engine access.
  • It is not a substitute for an independent audit, legal advice, or professional compliance judgment.
  • AI-assisted matches are not guaranteed to be complete or correct and should be reviewed before use.
  • IsoFlow does not guarantee audit success, certification, or compliance with a particular standard.

6. Scope, availability, and contact

Deliverables, document types, framework or control matrix, review level, turnaround, fees, and exclusions are agreed for each engagement. Do not assume a specific file-size limit, processing time, service-level commitment, or technical feature unless it is confirmed in writing.
