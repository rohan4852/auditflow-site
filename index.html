Frequently asked questions

Will Tier 2 or Tier 3 users access the processing tool directly?

No. In both models, IsoFlow supports project delivery behind the scenes. Participants receive results relevant to their project within the app, rather than direct access to a separate tool interface.

What are the three tiers?

Tier 1 connects businesses with independent practitioners, who deliver projects using their own tools and workflows. Tier 2 is planned for individual consultants using IsoFlow-supported project delivery. Tier 3 is planned for selected teams on an invitation-based basis. Tier 2 and Tier 3 are Coming Soon.

Who delivers Tier 1 projects?

The independent practitioner delivers the work agreed with the business, using their own tools, expertise and workflow. IsoFlow facilitates the connection and does not deliver Tier 1 projects.

What about data security?

Review the published security information and confirm the current processing arrangements with us before sharing sensitive client material. Do not submit confidential data until the handling terms have been agreed.

How are project scope and deliverables agreed?

For Tier 1, the business and independent practitioner discuss and agree the scope, deliverables, timeline and terms directly before work begins.

How do I get started?

Businesses can email their project requirements. Independent practitioners can share their expertise and preferred project types. Please do not attach confidential documents to the initial enquiry.
