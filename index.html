How the Orchestration Works

A strict 3-step pipeline to prevent hallucination and guarantee compliance formatting.

01

Ingest & Pre-Process

Drop your raw PDF/Word policies into the engine. The local pre-processor automatically handles messy tables, headers, and footers that break standard LLMs.

02

Strict Control Mapping

The orchestration layer cross-references your text against exact SOC 2 criteria or ISO 27001 Annex A controls, forcing the output into a strict JSON schema.

03

Export & Wipe

Download the fully formatted Excel audit trail. The moment the file is generated, the ephemeral instance is purged. No data is saved.
