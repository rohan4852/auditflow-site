Security & data handling

A clear view of document processing

IsoFlow helps compliance professionals review policy documents and organize potential evidence against control requirements. This page distinguishes the current demo from planned paid-service infrastructure.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Current vs. planned: The current demo uses Google Gemini for AI-assisted processing. A paid service using AWS and Amazon Bedrock is planned, but is not represented as deployed. Provider, configuration, and applicable terms must be confirmed for the specific service used.

1. Current document processing

The current demo is not connected to a database for persistent storage of uploaded documents. Files are intended to be temporary and used to process the session and produce the requested output.

This does not guarantee that every temporary copy, application or infrastructure log, diagnostic record, browser download, or third-party provider record is immediately erased. Actual handling can depend on hosting, operational configuration, and the services involved.

Please do not upload highly sensitive client evidence unless you have reviewed the current processing arrangements and are authorized to share it.

2. AI processing and third-party providers

Current demo

The demo may send document text or related processing inputs to Google Gemini for AI-assisted extraction and mapping. Google’s service terms, data-handling practices, and the configuration used may affect how that information is handled.

Planned paid service

The planned paid-service architecture is intended to use AWS and Amazon Bedrock. This is a roadmap item, not a statement that the service is already deployed or that a particular retention setting, contractual protection, or model configuration is active. Relevant details will be confirmed before that service is offered.

Other service providers may support website hosting, enquiry forms, operations, or requested features. Providers may differ by feature and change over time.

3. Transmission and access

Use the official HTTPS website when submitting information. Encryption and other safeguards depend on the systems and services involved; this page does not claim that every endpoint uses a particular TLS version or that every system has identical controls.

Access to submitted information should be limited to what is needed to operate the service and respond to a request. This overview is not an independent security audit, penetration test, certification, or service-level commitment.

4. What IsoFlow does not claim

  • No guarantee of zero data retention or instant purging across every system.
  • No claim that every provider retains no prompts, responses, logs, or operational records.
  • No claim that IsoFlow holds SOC 2, ISO 27001, or another security certification unless explicitly confirmed in a separate current statement.
  • No guarantee that AI-generated mappings are complete, accurate, or ready for audit submission without review.

5. Customer responsibilities

  • Confirm you have permission to submit documents and any personal or confidential information they contain.
  • Remove or redact information that is not necessary for the requested analysis.
  • Review extracted evidence, control mappings, and generated outputs before relying on them or sharing them with a client, auditor, or certification body.
  • Contact IsoFlow before sending sensitive material if you need to clarify processing arrangements for a particular engagement.

6. Questions or security concerns

For questions about document handling or to report a suspected security issue, contact rohan@isoflowai.in. Please do not email confidential compliance evidence unless the handling method has been agreed in advance.

This page is a high-level description, not a promise of a particular technical control or a substitute for written terms agreed for a specific engagement.
