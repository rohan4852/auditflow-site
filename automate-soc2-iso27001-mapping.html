Why evidence mapping takes work

Compliance teams often need to find policy statements that may support specific control requirements, trace those statements back to their source documents, and identify areas where additional evidence or review may be needed. This work can involve repeated reading, interpretation, and spreadsheet organization.

IsoFlow is designed to help organize this work—not to decide compliance status on your behalf.

From documents to reviewable evidence

1. Define the control scope

Agree on the framework, control matrix, documents, desired output, and review requirements for the engagement.

2. Identify potentially relevant statements

Document analysis helps locate text that may relate to the selected controls. The relevance of each candidate depends on the source content and the control interpretation.

3. Keep source references in view

Where available, results can include source excerpts and document or page references so reviewers can trace candidates back to the original material.

4. Review and refine

Human review helps inspect potential matches and prepare structured results. Reviewers should verify the original wording, references, control interpretation, and whether the evidence is sufficient for its intended use.

Benefits for compliance practitioners

  • Organize potential evidence against a supplied control matrix.
  • Reduce the need to manually assemble an initial list of candidate policy statements.
  • Keep source references visible where the input documents and output support them.
  • Produce structured results that can be reviewed and refined by your team.
  • Identify candidate gaps for further investigation without treating an absent match as proof of non-compliance.

Actual usefulness depends on document quality, control scope, source references, and review effort. No specific time saving or processing speed is promised.

Designed for human review—not automated audit conclusions

AI-assisted outputs can omit relevant evidence, misinterpret a statement, or suggest a match that does not satisfy the control. IsoFlow's results should be checked against the original documents and the applicable control requirements before they are relied on or shared with a client, auditor, or certification body.

IsoFlow does not provide certification or an independent audit opinion and does not guarantee audit outcomes.
