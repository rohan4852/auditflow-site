Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

At IsoFlow Systems ("we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our stateless evidence mapping engine.

2. Processing of Audit Documents (The Stateless Guarantee)

IsoFlow is explicitly designed as a stateless processor.

  • No Storage: The PDF, DOCX, and CSV files you upload are processed entirely in volatile memory (RAM).
  • Immediate Deletion: The moment your browser receives the generated Excel export, your session data is permanently destroyed. We do not retain, log, or backup your client documents.
  • No AI Training: Your documents are never used to train our AI models or third-party LLMs.

3. Information We Actually Collect

To provide our service, manage your account, and handle billing, we collect limited administrative data:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and professional title when you request pilot access.
  • Billing Information: Processed securely via our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage Metadata: Anonymous connection telemetry (e.g., number of mapping requests processed) to monitor API uptime.

4. Third-Party Subprocessors

We use trusted, SOC-2 compliant vendors to operate our infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform (hosting), Stripe (payments), and Tally (forms). We also interface with enterprise LLM APIs under strict zero-retention data processing agreements.
