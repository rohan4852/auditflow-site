Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how IsoFlow Systems ("IsoFlow", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you visit our website, use our document-processing service, contact us, submit a business project request, or apply to join our independent practitioner network. IsoFlow is designed for business and professional use.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use IsoFlow, we may collect the following information:

  • Documents and processing inputs: Files, control matrices, text, and related content you submit to generate compliance mappings and other requested outputs.
  • Business project requests: Organization name, contact name, email address, compliance framework, project summary, and any timeline or budget details you choose to provide.
  • Practitioner applications: Name, email address, professional role, skills, frameworks, experience or credentials you choose to share, availability, and preferred project types.
  • Communications: Information you include in emails, support requests, or other messages to us.
  • Technical information: Technical or diagnostic information that may be processed by the website, hosting, security, and other service providers when you access our services.

Please submit only information relevant to the service or request. Make sure you have the authority to provide any documents or information you submit. Avoid including passwords, access credentials, or personal information that is not needed for the requested work.

3. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes such as:

  • Processing submitted documents and generating requested compliance-related outputs.
  • Responding to questions and providing service-related communications.
  • Reviewing business project requests and practitioner applications.
  • Considering and facilitating relevant introductions between businesses and independent practitioners when appropriate.
  • Operating, maintaining, securing, and improving our website and services.
  • Preventing misuse, resolving disputes, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Submitting a project request or practitioner application does not guarantee acceptance, an introduction, a project assignment, paid work, or a particular business outcome.

4. Document Processing and Retention

The current demonstration service processes submitted documents to generate compliance mappings and related outputs. The current demonstration is not connected to a database for persistent storage of uploaded documents, and uploaded files are intended to be temporary and removed after the processing session.

Document content may be sent to the AI provider used by the demonstration to generate the requested output. The processing and retention of that content may also depend on the provider's service terms and configuration.

IsoFlow aims to handle submitted documents for the requested processing purpose. The statement that files are intended to be temporary does not mean every copy is guaranteed to disappear immediately from every system; technical files, logs, hosting systems, and service providers may have separate handling and retention processes. Please do not submit information unless you are authorized to share it and are comfortable using the current demonstration service.

5. AI Processing: Demo and Planned Paid Service

Current demo — Google Gemini: The current demonstration may transmit submitted document content to Google's Gemini service for AI processing. The handling of that content is subject to the applicable Google service terms, privacy terms, and configuration.

Planned paid service — Amazon Bedrock on AWS: IsoFlow plans to use Amazon Web Services (AWS), including Amazon Bedrock, for AI processing in its paid service. This is planned infrastructure and is not a statement that the paid environment is already deployed. The specific foundation model, AWS services, and processing configuration will be determined by the deployed architecture.

Before the paid service launches, this Policy will be reviewed against the actual processing and retention arrangements. We describe the demo and paid service separately because they may use different infrastructure and data-handling configurations.

6. Trusted Service Providers

IsoFlow uses third-party technology providers to operate its website, process documents, deliver AI functionality, transmit form submissions, and support relevant operational, payment, and security functions. Information is shared with a provider when needed for that provider to perform its role in delivering or supporting the service.

We work to limit information shared with service providers to what is relevant to their role. Providers may process information under their own applicable terms, policies, and configurations. Our providers and configurations may change as the service develops.

7. How Information Is Shared

Documents and information submitted for compliance analysis are used to provide the requested service. IsoFlow does not make customer compliance documents publicly available or share them with unrelated businesses for their independent use.

If you request a business introduction or practitioner connection, we may share relevant contact and project information with the prospective party to help assess or facilitate that introduction.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or lawful process, or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of our services.

IsoFlow does not sell personal information to data brokers.

8. Forms and Email Communications

Business project requests and practitioner applications may be submitted through a third-party form or email intake service and delivered to our designated email inbox for review. The provider processes the information you enter to transmit your submission.

Please do not use public intake forms to send confidential compliance evidence or documents. Information included in an email may remain in the relevant inboxes and provider systems until deleted, subject to available controls and applicable retention practices.

9. Retention and Deletion Requests

We retain business contact details, application information, and communications for as long as reasonably needed to respond to requests, manage relationships, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention can vary by information type and system.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to ask a question about how it is handled. We will review requests under applicable law and may need to verify your identity or authority. Deleting information from our own records may not immediately delete copies held by email, hosting, or other service providers; we will take reasonable steps where appropriate and available. Information may be retained where required or permitted by law.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information handled through our services. No online service or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Users can help protect their information by submitting only necessary materials, ensuring they have permission to share them, and avoiding the submission of passwords or unrelated sensitive data.

If you have a privacy or security concern about information submitted to IsoFlow, contact us using the details below so we can review it.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request information about personal data processing, seek correction or deletion, withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis for processing, or raise a privacy grievance. To make a request, contact us at the email address below. We will review and respond in accordance with applicable requirements.

12. Cookies and Technical Information

The website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies and process technical information for website functionality, security, and diagnostics. The specific information processed depends on the features and services enabled. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required.

13. Children's Privacy

IsoFlow is intended for business and professional use and is not designed for children. Please do not submit children's personal information unless you are legally authorized to do so and the information is necessary for a legitimate purpose. If you believe such information has been submitted inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, data practices, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date. Please review this page periodically, particularly when using new features or services.
